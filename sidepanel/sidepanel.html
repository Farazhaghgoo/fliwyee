How to use
  • Click a word in the subtitles
  • Drag across words for a phrase
  • S the whole subtitle line
  • R replay the line · Shift+R slower
  • Q ask your AI chat about the last capture
  • N add a note to the last capture, or fix it
  • Ctrl+Z undo the last capture

Pausing keeps the session going. Ending it (Ctrl+Shift+E or End session) saves it to Downloads/italian-subtitles.
