How to use
  • Click a word in the subtitles: its sentence is copied with the word in bold, and saved
  • Drag across words for a phrase
  • S the whole subtitle line
  • 1 Word · 2 Sentence / idiom · 3 Grammar structure · 4 Listening: tag the last capture
  • R replay the sentence · Shift+R slower
  • Q ask your AI chat about the last capture
  • N open these notes and edit the last capture
  • Ctrl+Z undo the last capture

Every video has its own notebook. Each click is saved in Fliwyee right away. The notebook file goes to Downloads/italian-subtitles when you press Ctrl+Shift+E, leave the video or close this panel.

Fliwyee runs by itself on YouTube (also players embedded in other pages) and Netflix. On any other video website, press Ctrl+Shift+X on that page to add it there.
